### Modifications for LoPRax

The downloaded GLB was modified for this project:

- hundreds of mesh parts were consolidated into a web-oriented single-mesh GLB;
- runtime centering and scale normalization were added;
- original neutral appearance is replaced by LoPRax royal vertex coloring and PBR material tuning;
- particle targets are sampled from the modified model surface;
- cinematic lighting, bloom, fog, and ember effects are added by the web application.

Attribution is also displayed directly in the deployed Lab UI.
